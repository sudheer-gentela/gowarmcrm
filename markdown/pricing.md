# GoWarm Pricing

> Two products on one platform, each with a flat monthly price for the team rather than a per-seat charge. GoWarm CRM covers sales execution. GoWarm Work covers projects and daily work. Buy either on its own, or both together for one combined price.

## At a glance

| Plan | Price | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| GoWarm CRM Growth | $999/month | Up to 20 reps and 5 manager seats |
| GoWarm Work | $999/month | Up to 25 users |
| GoWarm CRM and GoWarm Work combined | $1,499/month | Up to 25 users across both products |
| Enterprise (any of the above) | Custom | Above the limits shown |

Adding a person does not change the price until the plan's limit is passed.

## GoWarm CRM — sales execution

For when deals stall and nobody notices until the review. GoWarm CRM checks the pipeline every night and gives each rep a ranked list of what to do today.

### Growth — $999/month

Up to 20 reps and 5 manager seats. Includes:

- Every deal checked nightly for stalls and risk
- A ranked daily action list for each rep
- AI drafts the next step, and reps approve it
- Playbooks that run when a deal changes stage
- Forecast health and a manager overview
- Works on top of Salesforce or HubSpot, or as the organisation's CRM
- Onboarding and priority support

AI usage is billed separately, based on actual use, with the cost visible after every call.

### Enterprise — custom pricing

More than 20 reps. Everything in Growth, plus single sign-on (SSO/SAML), custom CRM integrations, multiple teams and regions, custom rules and thresholds, bring-your-own AI provider key, uptime SLA and quarterly reviews.

## GoWarm Work — projects and daily work

For when projects slip and nobody sees the work. GoWarm Work tracks projects against the plan, and each person logs one line a day.

### Work — $999/month

Up to 25 users. Includes:

- Projects with stages, gates and task dependencies
- Plan versus actual, against a baseline frozen at the start
- Evidence required before a task closes
- Timeboxed projects and standing work tracked separately
- One line a day per person, rolled up for managers
- Bill of Quantities: budgets, procurement and variations
- Separate schedules and holiday calendars per department
- Onboarding session and setup review

No timers, screenshots, idle detection or productivity scores. No sales modules need to be enabled.

### Work Enterprise — custom pricing

More than 25 users. Everything in Work, plus single sign-on (SSO/SAML), data migration and custom onboarding, multiple departments and sites, priority support and uptime SLA, and a named point of contact.

## Both products — $1,499/month

For organisations that win work and then deliver it. Up to 25 users across both products. Includes everything in GoWarm CRM Growth and everything in GoWarm Work, with won deals handing over to delivery projects. AI usage on the sales side is billed separately.

Above 25 users, pricing is custom and includes everything in both Enterprise plans.

## Common questions

**What happens if I cancel?**
You aren't charged again. The period you've already paid for isn't refunded.

**Does GoWarm CRM replace my CRM?**
If you already have a CRM, GoWarm CRM sits on top of it. Salesforce and HubSpot are supported today. If you don't have a CRM, you can use GoWarm CRM as your CRM.

**Do I have to buy both products?**
No. Each product runs on its own, and you can add the other later without migrating anything. If you want both, the combined plan is $1,499 a month for up to 25 users.

**Is AI usage included in the price?**
AI usage on GoWarm CRM is billed separately, based on what you actually use, and you can see the cost after every call. Enterprise customers can use their own AI provider key instead.

**Is there a free trial?**
For GoWarm CRM, yes. You can try it free with sample playbooks before connecting anything. For GoWarm Work, book a walkthrough and we'll set up one live project and one team while you watch.

**Does GoWarm Work monitor employees?**
No. There are no timers, screenshots, idle detection or productivity scores. A manager sees whether work was recorded on a working day, measured against that person's schedule and your holiday calendar.
