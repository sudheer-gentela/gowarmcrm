# GoWarm Pricing

> Two products on one platform, priced separately, both flat-rate by organisation rather than per seat. GoWarm CRM covers sales execution above your CRM. GoWarm Work covers projects and daily work. Either can be bought on its own — modules are enabled per organisation, so nothing you do not buy is switched on.

## GoWarm CRM — sales execution

Priced by reps, because reps are who work the queue.

### Growth — $999/month

- Billed annually (~$11,988/year)
- Up to 20 reps · 5 manager seats
- Flat rate: the price does not change with rep count inside the band

Includes:

- Nightly diagnostic engine across deals, prospects, contracts, cases and handovers
- Live action queue for every rep, ranked by what matters now
- AI-drafted next actions, with rep approval required
- Unlimited playbooks and play chaining
- Standard CRM connectors: Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Dynamics
- Email and calendar signal ingestion
- Contract and CLM action queues
- Prospecting hurdle scores and handover commitment tracking
- Forecast health dashboard, manager action overview, RevOps view
- Diagnostic rule customization and per-user AI settings
- Priority support and onboarding

AI usage is metered separately, visible after every call, billed on actual usage on top of the plan fee.

### Enterprise — custom pricing

Above 20 reps. Everything in Growth, plus custom integrations, multi-team and multi-region configuration, bring-your-own AI key, SSO and SAML, custom rules and threshold tuning, dedicated onboarding, uptime SLA, and quarterly business reviews.

## GoWarm Work — projects and daily work

Priced by people who log work, because delivery is not measured in reps.

### Work — $999/month

- Billed annually (~$11,988/year)
- Up to 25 users
- No sales module needs to be enabled

Includes:

- Stages with gates, task dependencies with cycle detection
- Baseline frozen at start, with plan-versus-actual drift measured against it
- Evidence required before a task closes
- Timeboxed and standing work tracked separately, so a retainer is not a project stuck at 90 per cent
- My day, controlled activity vocabulary, reminders, backfill window
- Compliance measured against working days and your holiday calendar, not against generated tasks
- Manager rollup and per-person timeline
- Bill of Quantities with an append-only spend ledger, procurement and variations
- Departments with separate working schedules and holiday calendars
- Conversation attachment on the channels your organisation chooses
- Document storage connections
- Onboarding session and setup review

No timers, screenshots, idle detection or productivity scores. The measure is whether work was recorded on a working day.

### Work Enterprise — custom pricing

Above 25 users. Everything in Work, plus single sign-on, custom onboarding and data migration, multi-department and multi-site configuration, priority support and uptime SLA, and a named point of contact.

## Both plans are $999. They are not the same thing.

GoWarm CRM Growth covers up to 20 reps on the sales side. GoWarm Work covers up to 25 users on the delivery side. They are separate products and buying one does not include the other. An organisation that wants both should ask for a combined price rather than paying twice — a services firm that sells and delivers usually should.

## Trial

The free trial runs on sample playbooks and applies to the sales side only. It does not extend to a projects-only organisation. For GoWarm Work, book a walkthrough instead: one live project and one team, set up while you watch.
