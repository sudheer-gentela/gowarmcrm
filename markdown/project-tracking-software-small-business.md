# Project Tracking Software for Small Business

> Project tracking for companies of up to about 500 people that have no project office. Every mainstream project tool assumes someone maintains it — a project office, a scrum master, an admin. Under 500 people that person does not exist, so the plan drifts out of date and the team returns to a spreadsheet. GoWarm Work puts the discipline in the structure instead.

## Why project tools fail in small companies

Updating a project tool is a separate task, and separate tasks compete with the work they describe. The record is therefore maintained best when the team is idle and worst when the team is under pressure — which is exactly when the information matters most.

The second failure does more damage. Most tools generate work: recurring tasks, reminders, alerts, weekly digests. Generated items arrive on a schedule; attention does not. Once the queue passes the length that can be read in a sitting it stops being read at all, and the tool is reporting on itself rather than on your projects.

Neither failure is fixed by training or by changing vendor. Both are structural.

## What replaces the administrator

- **Gates hold the next stage shut.** A stage opens when the current one is genuinely met, not when the calendar says so.
- **Dependencies are checked at creation.** Task dependencies are validated for circularity when drawn, not discovered when the schedule will not resolve.
- **The baseline is frozen at the start.** Drift becomes a measured difference. A plan re-fitted each month cannot be wrong, which also means it cannot tell you anything.
- **Evidence closes a task.** A file, a link or a note that cannot be quietly rewritten, rather than the owner declaring it done.

## Work that never finishes

Two independent axes: customer or internal, and timeboxed or standing. All four combinations are ordinary — an implementation with a go-live, an internal migration with an owner and a date, a managed-service retainer, and a standing capability such as training or compliance.

Tools that only model finite projects force the standing half into a permanently open item, which then distorts percentage complete, overdue counts and projects-at-risk. In GoWarm Work standing work is retired rather than completed and stays out of delivery statistics, while remaining tracked and logged against.

## Bill of Quantities

A first-class part of the project rather than an export. Progress is recorded as increments rather than running totals. Every entry snapshots the rate it was booked at. A mistake is corrected by a reversal pointing at the original, because a spend ledger that can be edited is not a ledger. Approved variations stay separate from the original bill, so the reason a project grew is legible rather than absorbed.

## Who it fits

Companies up to about 500 people in any industry: construction and interiors, engineering and manufacturing services, IT services and agencies, professional services, facilities and field service, and internal functions such as data, marketing, finance and delivery.
